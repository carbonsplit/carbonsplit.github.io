## 2026-07-28 - Accessible Tooltip Triggers for Absolute Interactive Elements
**Learning:** Tooltips or utility buttons (like "Copy") that rely entirely on CSS hover states (e.g., `group-hover:opacity-100`) are completely hidden and unreachable for screen readers and keyboard-only users. By pairing hover modifiers with focus-within triggers (such as Tailwind's `group-focus-within`), we ensure that when a user tabs onto the interactive element, the hidden controls smoothly reveal themselves. Additionally, interactive buttons within these triggers must have robust focus indicator rings and dynamic, self-describing ARIA attributes to announce their states (e.g., "Copy email address" vs "Email address copied").
**Action:** Always combine `group-hover` with `group-focus-within` and use focus-visible styles alongside dynamic, clear `aria-label` or `aria-describedby` attributes for tooltip-styled trigger interactions.
